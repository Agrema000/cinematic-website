Guest Information

Hotel Policies & Rules.

Review our property guidelines to ensure a smooth, comfortable, and secure stay for all guests at Jayhiltin Hotel & Suites.

Parking Guidelines

Self-parking: Available on-site and strictly complimentary for all registered hotel guests.

Secured Compound: Our parking bays are located within a fully gated perimeter equipped with 24/7 security surveillance and dedicated gate personnel.

Wi-Fi & Connectivity

High-Speed Access: Complimentary high-speed wireless internet access is provided across all guest rooms, suites, lobby lounge, and poolside areas.

Access Credentials: Network passwords and login tokens are issued directly by the front desk upon check-in confirmation.

Check-in & Check-out Protocols

Standard Check-in Time: 2:00 PM onwards.

Standard Check-out Time: 12:00 PM noon.

Late Departures: Subject to availability and may incur a nominal administrative charge upon prior arrangement with the front desk.

Smoking Guidelines

Non-Smoking Rooms: All indoor guest rooms, suites, and indoor lounge areas are strictly non-smoking to maintain optimal air quality.

Designated Areas: Smoking is permitted exclusively in designated open-air terrace zones and the outdoor bar grounds.
